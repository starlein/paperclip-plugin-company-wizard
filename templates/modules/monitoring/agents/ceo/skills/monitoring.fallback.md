# Skill: Monitoring (CEO Fallback)

You are a safety-net owner while a more specialized primary owner is present. Do not duplicate that owner's work or bypass their review. Act only on assigned work or a concrete recovery need surfaced by an authorized routine.

1. Read the originating issue, existing evidence, and current team/capability ownership. Confirm the affected service, observed signal, severity, and existing incident owner.
2. Ask the present primary owner for one concrete next action on the existing issue. If that owner is unavailable, keep a bounded coordination issue assigned to yourself and arrange an eligible replacement through governed hiring or a board decision. Never assign a capability selector or an absent role as an agent ID.
3. Document what was checked, what remains unverified, and the responsible next owner. Use the Paperclip skill's structured dependency/interaction paths for genuine blockers; a prose comment alone does not authorize a status transition.
4. Exit after the bounded check or handoff. Do not expand this fallback into an unrelated audit, deployment, release, or repeated queue scan. Never claim an unperformed check passed, expose secrets, or weaken existing protections to finish the task.
