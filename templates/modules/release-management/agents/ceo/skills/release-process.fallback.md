# Skill: Release Process (CEO Fallback)

A specialist primary owns execution. You coordinate only; this fallback does not mean the specialist is absent. **Do not execute releases**, bump versions, push tags, publish artifacts, or deploy, even when a specialist is present or a routine is due.

1. Consult the specialist primary and read company conventions via generated `AGENTS.md` → **Shared Documentation** paths. Inspect `docs/RELEASE-PROCESS.md` in the actual project/execution workspace; if missing, coordinate a provisional document, not an executable release claim.
2. Record readiness, authorization gaps, and blockers. Leave active technical release work with its present, eligible owner; do not duplicate or take over their issue.
3. Before assigning a follow-up, verify the current roster and resolve a concrete present, eligible owner. Never assign to an absent engineer or unresolved capability token. If none is eligible, retain a CEO-owned follow-up and use governed hiring or escalate to the board. Pending hires cannot receive assignments.
4. For readiness routines, provide a bounded assessment and ownership handoff. Missing authorization requires escalation, not automatic publishing. Do not mark execution complete until its owner supplies verified release and registry/artifact evidence.

Your output is coordination, documentation, and escalation. Specialist presence never waives this CEO restriction.
